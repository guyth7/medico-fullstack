6. Important Firestore Collections

adminsAdministrator permissions
website_usersApproved staff profiles
staff_requestsPending, approved, and rejected staff registrations
patientsPatient profiles and nested medical records
admin_auditAdministrator activity history

Patient documents may contain the following nested collections:

  • prescriptions
  • diagnoses
  • allergies
  • surgeries
  • lab
  • radiology
  • fileChunks

Administrator patient deletion recursively removes the patient and these associated records.

7. Registration and Permissions

Supported staff roles:

  • Doctor
  • Hospital
  • Laboratory
  • Pharmacy

New staff accounts register through the website, receive a pending status, and cannot enter their dashboard until an administrator approves the request. The Admin page is protected by the admins/{uid} Firestore authorization record; knowing the URL does not bypass security.
